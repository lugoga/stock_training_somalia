## Response Time Guidelines

- **Initial acknowledgment**: Within 24 hours
- **Registration decision**: Within 48-72 hours
- **Pre-training communications**: 2-4 weeks before program
- **Final logistics**: 1 week before program

## Tips for Coordinators

1. **Personalize responses** - Use participant's name and reference their specific interests
2. **Be prompt** - Respond within 24-48 hours
3. **Provide clear next steps** - Include dates, actions needed, and contacts
4. **Include resources** - Link to relevant materials and information
5. **Set expectations** - Clear timelines and what to expect
6. **Follow up** - Track confirmations and send reminders
7. **Regional coordination** - CC regional coordinators as appropriate

## Common Questions to Address

1. **Program costs** - Clarify any fees, sponsorship, or subsidies
2. **Prerequisites** - Educational background, experience requirements
3. **Certificate** - Type of certificate, requirements to earn it
4. **Language** - Primary language of instruction, translation available
5. **Accommodation** - Provided, self-arranged, or recommendations
6. **Travel** - Transportation arrangements, reimbursement
7. **Materials** - What's provided, what to bring
8. **Technology** - Laptop requirements, software needed

## Escalation Path

For complex inquiries or issues:
1. **Regional coordinators** - Region-specific questions
2. **TAFIRI training lead** - Technical content questions
3. **Program director** - Policy or strategic questions
4. **Administrative team** - Logistics, travel, accommodation
